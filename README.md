# Welcome to my personal hobby GitHub!

It contains projects I build in my spare time.

## Current projects being worked on

- **[McChess](https://github.com/MecGlandorff/McChess)** — A small neural chess engine trained from scratch on human games, without engine supervision. Its neural network guides a limited MCTS search instead of Stockfish’s alpha-beta approach. Trained on a local **RTX 4060** GPU, with a **rough local Stockfish-UCI handicap estimate of 2653 ELO** across 200 games.

- **[News](https://github.com/MecGlandorff/News)** — a local-first AI news intelligence system that turns noisy feeds into source-grounded, evolving story memory.

- **[Kanon](https://github.com/MecGlandorff/Kanon)** - a skill that gives you, as an engineer, full overview of the systems you are working on.

- **[Maths](https://github.com/MecGlandorff/maths)** - a repo in which different small math projects are written out. Practicing and keeping math & C++ fresh.
